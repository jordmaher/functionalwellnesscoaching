The Seed Sessions · One session at a time

One thing on your mind. One session to talk it through.

A decision you keep going back and forth on, a conversation you're dreading, a week that got away from you. Book a single session whenever you need one. We look at what's in front of you, find what's really in the way, and you leave knowing your next step.

This is for you if

You have something specific on your mind and want to think it through with someone, without signing up for a program. Or you've already worked with me and want to come back for a check-in when a new season asks something of you.

This may not be the right fit if

You feel stuck in many places at once and want steady support week to week (Taking Root or The Full Season may fit better), or you need clinical support right now. If that's the case, I'm glad to help you find the right person.

How a session goes

Look, sort, choose.

To begin

Look

We start with curiosity. What's on your mind, why it matters right now, and what you've already tried. I ask questions and listen closely, so the reasons and strengths you already have come to the surface.

In the middle

Sort

We name what's really in the way: time, energy, fear of getting it wrong, other people's expectations. Then we look at your options and what each one would ask of you.

Before we finish

Choose

You pick your next step, and we write it down together in your own words. You leave with a short list you can act on this week, not a pile of homework.

What you could bring

Whatever is on your mind.

  • A job decision or career move you keep putting off
  • A hard conversation you want to walk into prepared
  • A project that hasn't left your head yet
  • Routines, energy, or focus that keep slipping
  • A check-in after Taking Root or The Full Season, when life changes

What's included

Simple and flexible.

  • A short form before your first session, so we start where you actually are
  • One 50-minute session, one-on-one and online
  • Your next steps, written together before we finish
  • Book another session whenever you need one, with no commitment

You leave with

A clearer head, a better sense of what's been in the way, and a next step that feels like yours.

How it works

Four simple steps.

  • 1. Book a free 20-minute call so we can make sure it fits.
  • 2. Fill out a short form about what's on your mind.
  • 3. Meet online for 50 minutes.
  • 4. Come back whenever you'd like. One session or several, at your pace.

Questions

Before you book.

Can I book more than one?

Yes. Book one session, or come back as often as it's helpful. There's no package to buy and nothing to commit to.

Is one session enough?

For one clear question, often it is. If we find there's more underneath, we'll talk about whether more sessions, Taking Root, or The Full Season would help. It's always your call.

How is this different from Taking Root?

The Seed Sessions are one at a time, with no plan between them. Taking Root is four weekly sessions plus email check-ins, for when you want steady support while you make a change.

What do I need to prepare?

Just the short form and a quiet place to talk. Have something to write with if you like.

What if I need to move a session?

Just let me know 24 hours ahead and we'll find a new time. Sessions missed without notice count as used.

Is this therapy?

No. I'm not a therapist or a doctor, and this isn't clinical treatment. It's one-on-one guidance focused on where you want to go. If something comes up that needs clinical care, I'll help you find the right support.

Is it virtual?

Yes. All sessions are online, so you can join from wherever you are.

What does it cost?

We'll talk through it on your free call.
