Work with me

Three ways to go from stuck to moving.

Every option is one-on-one and fully online. Choose the one that fits what's on your mind right now: a single session when you need one, a month of steady support, or a full season to go further.

This is for you if

You're capable and self-aware, and you still feel stuck. You're tired of surface fixes. You want someone in your corner who will listen closely, ask good questions, and be honest with you.

This may not be the right fit if

You're looking for someone to tell you exactly what to do, or you need clinical support right now. If that's the case, I'm glad to help you find the right person.

The Seed Sessions

What could you bring to a session?

  • A job decision or career move you keep putting off
  • A hard conversation you want to walk into prepared
  • A project that hasn't left your head yet
  • Routines, energy, or focus that keep slipping
  • A check-in after Taking Root or The Full Season, when life changes

The Full Season

Three phases, one direction.

Weeks 1 to 3

The Root Audit

An honest look at where you are. We map your patterns across work, relationships, energy, and rest, and name what's really keeping you in place. You'll start with a detailed intake form that we turn into a one-page picture of where you are.

Weeks 4 to 8

Building the Blueprint

We get clear on what you want, underneath what you think you should want. Then we design a path that fits your real life: decisions to make, conversations to have, boundaries to set, and habits to support them.

Weeks 9 to 12

Rooted in Action

You take the steps and we refine as life responds. By the end you trust yourself when change comes, and you have tools you can return to whenever a new season asks something of you.

Taking Root follows the same arc in four weeks, focused on one area of your life.

Questions

Before you reach out.

Which option is right for me?

If you have one specific thing you want to talk through, start with a Seed Session. If one area of your life needs steady progress and someone checking in, Taking Root fits. If you feel stuck in more than one place, or you want lasting change, The Full Season gives us room. Not sure? That's what the free call is for.

Can I start small and keep going?

Yes. You can begin with a Seed Session or Taking Root and continue into The Full Season if it feels right. And after any program, you can book a Seed Session whenever you'd like a check-in. We'll talk about it together; there's no pressure either way.

Is this therapy?

No. I'm not a therapist or a doctor, and this isn't clinical treatment. It's practical, one-on-one guidance focused on what comes next. Many women do this alongside therapy. If something comes up that needs clinical care, I'll help you find the right support.

What if I don't know what I want yet?

That's exactly where most people start. Not knowing is part of being stuck. Finding out is part of the work.

Is it virtual?

Yes. All sessions are online, so you can join from wherever you are.

What does it cost?

We'll talk through options on your free call, once we both know whether this is the right fit.
