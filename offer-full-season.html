The Full Season · 12 weeks, weekly

Twelve weeks for the deeper work.

For when the stuck feeling shows up in more than one place: your work, your relationships, your energy, and the bigger question of what comes next. Over twelve weeks we look at the whole picture, get clear on what you actually want, and build the habits and self-trust to get there.

Book your free intro call

This is for you if

You're capable and self-aware, and you still feel stuck, maybe in more than one area. You're tired of surface fixes and ready for deeper change. You want someone in your corner for a full season who will listen closely, ask good questions, and be honest with you.

This may not be the right fit if

You have one specific thing to talk through (The Seed Sessions may fit better), you want support on one area for a month (Taking Root may fit better), or you need clinical support right now. If that's the case, I'm glad to help you find the right person.

The 12 weeks

Three phases, one direction.

Weeks 1 to 3

The Root Audit

An honest look at where you are. We map your patterns across work, relationships, energy, and rest, and name what's really keeping you in place. You'll start with a detailed intake form that we turn into a one-page picture of where you are.

Weeks 4 to 8

Building the Blueprint

We get clear on what you want, underneath what you think you should want. Then we design a path that fits your real life: decisions to make, conversations to have, boundaries to set, and habits to support them.

Weeks 9 to 12

Rooted in Action

You take the steps and we refine as life responds. By the end you trust yourself when change comes, and you have tools you can return to whenever a new season asks something of you.

Your one-page picture

All of you, on one page.

Before we begin, you'll fill out an intake about your whole life, not just the part that feels stuck: work, relationships, sleep, energy, rest, and the habits that shape your days. I turn it into a one-page picture of where you are, so we can see the patterns together.

It isn't a diagnosis. It's a starting point, and it often shows connections you couldn't see from inside your week. If something comes up that's better handled by a doctor, therapist, or other professional, I'll help you find the right person and get connected.

What's included

A full season of support.

  • A detailed intake form, turned into a one-page picture of where you are
  • Twelve weekly one-on-one sessions, online
  • Email check-ins between sessions, Monday to Friday, so you're not alone midweek
  • A written plan you build in phase two and refine in phase three
  • A closing session focused on what to return to when a new season asks something of you

You leave with

  • A clear, honest picture of what's been keeping you stuck
  • A direction that's yours, not borrowed from someone else's expectations
  • Language and confidence for the hard conversations
  • A few small habits you can actually keep, for your energy and focus
  • A way to notice when you're stuck again, and a way through it

How it works

Five simple steps.

  • 1. Book a free 20-minute call so we can both see if it's the right fit.
  • 2. Sign up and choose a weekly time that works for you.
  • 3. Fill out the detailed intake form before our first session.
  • 4. Meet weekly for twelve weeks, with check-ins between sessions.
  • 5. Finish with your plan and your tools, ready for whatever comes next.

Questions

Before you reach out.

Why start with a call?

Twelve weeks is a real commitment, for both of us. A short call lets you ask anything and make sure this feels right before you begin.

What if I'm stuck in more than one area?

You probably are. Career, relationships, and the bigger question of who you're becoming tend to be tangled together. We work with the whole picture and start wherever feels most pressing.

What if I don't know what I want yet?

That's exactly where most people start. Not knowing is part of being stuck. Finding out is part of the work.

How long is each session?

50 minutes, once a week.

What do the check-ins between sessions look like?

Short emails to share how a step went, ask a question, or get a nudge when you're stalling. I read and reply Monday to Friday, within one business day.

What if I miss a week?

Life happens. Give me at least 24 hours' notice and we'll reschedule. Sessions missed without notice count as used.

Is this therapy?

No. I'm not a therapist or a doctor, and this isn't clinical treatment. It's practical, one-on-one guidance focused on what comes next. Many women do this alongside therapy. If something comes up that needs clinical care, I'll help you find the right support.

Is it virtual?

Yes. All sessions are online, so you can join from wherever you are.

What does it cost?

We'll talk through options on your free call.
