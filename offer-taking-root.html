Taking Root · One month, weekly

A month to get moving again.

You know something needs to change, and you're ready to act, just not alone. Over four weeks we focus on one area of your life, get honest about what's keeping you in place, and start taking steps while you have someone in your corner.

Book your free intro call

This is for you if

You want real progress on one area of your life (work, a relationship, your routines, a transition) and you know you follow through better with someone checking in. You're ready to try things between sessions, not just talk about them.

This may not be the right fit if

You have one specific thing to talk through and don't need weekly support (The Seed Sessions may fit better), you feel stuck in many areas at once and want room for deeper change (The Full Season may fit better), or you need clinical support right now. If that's the case, I'm glad to help you find the right person.

The four weeks

Look, clarify, design, practice.

The same path as The Full Season, focused on one area and paced for a month.

  • Week one: Look honestly. We go through your intake together: where you are, what you want, and what you've already tried. Then we choose the one area we'll focus on for the month.
  • Week two: Get clear. What you want underneath the "shoulds." We name the patterns behind the stuck feeling, like perfectionism, overcommitting, or running on empty, and notice the strengths you already bring.
  • Week three: Design. We build your plan: decisions to make, conversations to have, boundaries to set, and small habits to support them. You take your first step before we meet again.
  • Week four: Practice and look ahead. We look at what happened, adjust what didn't fit, and set you up to keep going on your own, with a plan for when you get stuck again.

Where you might focus

One area, your pick.

  • Work: a role that no longer fits, a next step, or how you show up
  • A relationship that needs a different conversation or boundary
  • Routines, energy, and focus that keep slipping
  • A transition you're in the middle of, like a move, a new chapter, or a loss of direction
  • The pull of perfectionism, and doing things before they feel "ready"

What's included

Support while you do the work.

  • An intake form before our first session
  • Four weekly one-on-one sessions, online
  • Email check-ins between sessions, Monday to Friday, so you're not alone midweek
  • A written 30-day plan you build and start during the month

You leave with

Clarity on what's been in the way, a plan that fits your real life, and a few weeks of proof that you can follow through.

How it works

Five simple steps.

  • 1. Book a free 20-minute call so we can make sure it fits.
  • 2. Fill out the intake form so we start where you actually are.
  • 3. Meet weekly for four sessions online.
  • 4. Check in between sessions when you need a nudge or want to share a win.
  • 5. Finish with your plan and a clear sense of how to keep going.

Questions

Before you start.

How is this different from The Seed Sessions?

The Seed Sessions are single sessions you book when you need one, with nothing in between. Taking Root gives you a month of support while you actually make the change, with time to try things, notice what happens, and adjust.

How long is each session?

50 minutes, once a week.

What do the check-ins between sessions look like?

Short emails to share how a step went, ask a question, or get a nudge when you're stalling. I read and reply Monday to Friday, within one business day.

What if I miss a week?

Life happens. Give me at least 24 hours' notice and we'll reschedule. Sessions missed without notice count as used.

What if I don't know which area to focus on?

That's what week one is for. We'll look at the whole picture and choose the place where change would help most.

Is this therapy?

No. I'm not a therapist or a doctor, and this isn't clinical treatment. It's practical, one-on-one guidance focused on what comes next. If something comes up that needs clinical care, I'll help you find the right support.

Is it virtual?

Yes. All sessions are online, so you can join from wherever you are.

What if I want to keep going after the month?

You can continue into The Full Season if it feels right. We'll talk about it together; there's no pressure either way.

What does it cost?

We'll talk through it on your free call.
